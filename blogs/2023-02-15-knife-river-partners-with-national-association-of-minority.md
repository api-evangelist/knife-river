PORTLAND, Ore. — Feb. 15, 2023 — Knife River Corporation announced today that it has partnered with the Oregon Chapter of the National Association of Minority Contractors (NAMC-Oregon) to provide $250,000 in training scholarships for historically underrepresented groups. The scholarships can be used for individuals to take classes at the Knife River Training Center in Albany, Oregon.
